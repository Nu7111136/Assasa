This policy explains how cancelling and refunds work for Rakhza, sold by {{ biz.name }}.

1. Try before you pay

Every new account gets 5 days free. No payment is taken for the trial, so you can decide whether Rakhza suits your shop before paying anything.

2. No automatic charges, cancel any time

Rakhza is Rs 299 for one calendar month, paid by you each month. We never charge you automatically. To cancel, simply do not pay for the next month; nothing else is needed. Your paid month keeps working until its end date.

3. When we refund

A month that has started is normally not refundable. We will refund you in full or in part when:

  • you paid twice for the same month by mistake, or paid more than Rs 299: we refund the extra;
  • money left your JazzCash but your month did not start, and we cannot start it for you;
  • Rakhza did not work for you because of a fault on our side for more than 3 days in a row during your paid month, and we could not fix it: we refund the unused days;
  • we close your account without you breaking our Terms: we refund the unused days.

If a JazzCash payment fails but money was deducted, JazzCash usually reverses it by itself. If it has not come back within 3 working days, contact us.

4. How to ask for a refund

  1. Contact us within 7 days of the payment, from your registered mobile number.
  2. Tell us the transaction ID (TID) or JazzCash reference and the date. Never send your PIN or OTP.
  3. We reply within 3 working days. If the refund is approved, we send it back to the JazzCash account it came from within 7 working days.
