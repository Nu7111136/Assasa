This policy explains what information {{ biz.name }} ("Rakhza", "we") collects when you use rakhza.digital, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we need to run the app.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name, mobile number, type of business and chosen language.
  • MPIN: stored only as a one-way scrambled code (a hash). Nobody at Rakhza can read your MPIN.
  • Your stock records: item names, units, prices, quantities, notebooks, and the history of stock in and out.
  • Payments: the transaction ID or JazzCash reference, amount, date and status, and the response code JazzCash sends us. We do not receive or store your JazzCash PIN, OTP, card or wallet balance.
  • Technical: your IP address, browser type, the pages you open and the time, kept in our server logs for security and fixing problems. These logs never contain your MPIN or your stock entries.

We do not ask for your CNIC, address or email to use Rakhza.

2. Why we use it

  • to create and run your account and show you your stock;
  • to manage your free trial and paid months, and to check payments;
  • to keep Rakhza safe, for example limiting repeated wrong MPIN attempts;
  • to help you when you contact us, such as resetting your MPIN;
  • to meet legal and accounting duties.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Who we share it with

  • JazzCash (Jazz / Mobilink Microfinance Bank), when you pay: the payment reference and amount needed to process and confirm it. JazzCash handles your payment under its own privacy terms.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the servers where Rakhza and its database are stored.
  • Google Fonts: pages load fonts from Google, so your browser sends Google your IP address when loading them.
  • Authorities, only when Pakistani law requires us to.

4. Cookies

Rakhza uses one essential cookie to keep you logged in and remember your language. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you block cookies, you cannot log in.

5. How we protect it

All traffic uses HTTPS encryption. MPINs are hashed. Access to the database is limited to the people who run Rakhza. Each shop can only see its own records. No system is perfectly safe, so keep your MPIN secret and tell us if you think someone used your account.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and stock records: while your account exists.
  • Payment records: as long as needed for our accounts and the law, even after an account is deleted.
  • Server logs: for a limited time, then deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and stock records. Contact us from your registered mobile number so we can confirm it is you. We will act within 30 days. Payment records we must keep by law are not deleted.

8. Children

Rakhza is for shop owners and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, show a notice in the app.
